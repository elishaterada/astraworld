# Product vision

## Player and promise

Astraworld is for a small group of friends who want to open a browser link and discover a colorful wilderness together. Its identity combines the exploration/settlement rhythm discussed through Valheim, the readability of Sephiria, and the personable creature companionship discussed through Dragon Quest Monsters and Dragon Quest Builders 2. These are experiential references, not asset or branding templates.

The emotional sequence is curiosity → danger → understanding a creature → companionship → a new possibility. A creature is valuable because it changes what the player can do. Combat-only pets and a collection checklist are insufficient to prove the product.

## Priorities

| Priority | Player outcome | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Join a friend with little friction | Independent browser sessions enter the same world |
| P0 | Understand danger and controls | Players identify attack tells and interaction targets without coaching |
| P0 | Experience companion utility | Players connect taming the Slime with opening the Forest route |
| P0 | Trust shared consequences | Both players see the same depletion, ownership and opened gate |
| P1 after slice | Return to meaningful progress | M6 restart restores inventory, companion and world edits |
| P1 after persistence | Build an attachment to home | M7–M8 gathering and crafting support a small settlement |

## Validation plan

Run the slice with at least three pairs unfamiliar with the implementation. Record time to join, time to first interaction, time to tame, time to open the gate, abandonment points and brief qualitative feedback. The provisional target is a 10–15 minute loop, with at least two of three pairs completing without intervention. This is a directional usability check, not a statistical proof of demand.

Ask after play: what did the companion enable, what would you do next, and what felt unclear? If players cannot name the utility, improve feedback before adding species. If networking feels unreliable, repair it before increasing player count.

## Release boundaries

The session prototype validates appeal. The persistent cooperative release adds recoverable progress for four players. A broader survival/crafting game follows only after those gates. Monetization, retention campaigns, public matchmaking, moderation tooling and economies are not part of the current package's implementation scope.
